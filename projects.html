Projects

Here are a few of my key projects that showcase my technical expertise and problem-solving skills.

1. AI-Powered Knowledge Engine for Smart Support & Ticket Resolution

Developed an intelligent AI-based knowledge management platform for customer support teams, leveraging LLMs like OpenAI GPT and Meta LLaMA for real-time recommendations and automated tagging.

  • Automatically classifies and tags support articles using semantic understanding (NLP).
  • Provides real-time solution recommendations based on live ticket content.
  • Detects knowledge gaps by analyzing article usage and reporting low-coverage topics.
  • Integrates with Google Sheets for ticket data and Slack for team alerts and updates.
  • Improves response consistency, reduces resolution time, and enhances content quality.

2. Fitness Tracker App

Implemented data cleaning, preprocessing, and model training pipelines with scikit-learn. Deployed an interactive Streamlit web app allowing users to preview data, choose models, and view real-time prediction results with accuracy metrics.
